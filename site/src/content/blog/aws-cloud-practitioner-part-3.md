AWS publishes six advantages and the exam quotes them almost verbatim, so it
is worth knowing them as phrases rather than as concepts you can paraphrase.

## Trade capital expense for variable expense

You stop buying servers ahead of demand and start paying for what you consume.
The exam wording is "capital expense" and "variable expense", not CapEx and
OpEx.

## Benefit from massive economies of scale

Aggregate demand across all AWS customers produces lower per-unit prices than
any single organisation could negotiate.

## Stop guessing capacity

Capacity planning becomes a runtime decision rather than a procurement one.
This is the advantage most often confused with elasticity in questions.

## Increase speed and agility

New resources are minutes away rather than weeks, which changes what
experiments are worth running.

## Stop spending money running and maintaining data centres

Racking, cooling, and physical security stop being your problem.

## Go global in minutes

Deploying into additional Regions is a configuration change, not a project.
